---
title: Closing Issues is Therapeutic
---

This week started pretty slow. I was getting blocked in OpenWISP again and
again. It was just by the end of the week when work started to pace through.

I was relieved with the Selenium tests PR getting merged. Making those tests
work consistently on CI was a huge pain, I am glad that it has been taken care
of now.

I also came across the `SIGUSR1` system signal this week while adding a feature
in the openwisp-controller. This made me learn about other system signals in
Linux. Before this, the only one I knew about was `SIGTERM`.

I also opened PRs for three other small bugs in OpenWISP. Working on these
small issues brought back the pace I was craving for. The notification for
PRs getting merged and issues getting closed bring me immense joy. This joy
keeps me going.
